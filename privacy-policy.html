Privacy Policy

This page explains what data we collect when you visit The Sleep SOP, how we use it, and what your rights are. Last updated: August 2026.

The Sleep SOP August 2026

Who we are

The Sleep SOP (thesleepsop.com) is a science-backed sleep improvement resource. To get in touch, visit our contact page.

What data we collect

1. Cookies

We use two types of cookies. Essential cookies remember your cookie preference so we don't ask every visit. Analytics cookies (only with your consent) use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages visited, time spent, device type. All data is anonymised. We never sell it.

2. Advertising

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings.

3. Affiliate links

We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click a product link and buy on Amazon, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. Amazon may set cookies to track these purchases. We also participate in other affiliate programmes. Commissions never influence our editorial content. All affiliate links are labelled on the page.

4. Newsletter

If you subscribe, we collect your email address via Formspree to send sleep-related updates. We don't share it with third parties. Unsubscribe any time via the link in any email.

5. Contact form

If you contact us, we collect your name and email solely to reply. This information is not stored beyond the correspondence.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Consent — analytics and advertising cookies, loaded only after you accept. Legitimate interests — essential cookies. Contract — newsletter delivery.
